Model Distillation Best Practices

Xuehan Xiong, Youbao Tang, Fei Xia, Bao Thach, Joseph Pagadora

The first post in a series on off-policy distillation. We fine-tune Qwen3 base models (1.7B–14B) on teacher-generated reasoning traces for math, instruction following, ARC-AGI 1, and τ²-bench.
